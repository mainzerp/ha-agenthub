==========================
3. AGENT CATALOG — POSITIVE / NEGATIVE EXAMPLES
==========================

Note: entity and device names below are opaque proper names (Rules 1+2);
they appear verbatim in the user's own language.

- light-agent (lights, switches, plugs):
  YES "Turn on the kitchen light" → light-agent: turn on kitchen light
  YES "Turn on Ambiente Wohnen" → light-agent: turn on Ambiente Wohnen
      ("Ambiente Wohnen" is a device name, not a scene — Rules 1+2)
  NO  "Activate the movie night scene" → scene-agent, NOT light-agent

- scene-agent (Home Assistant scene entities only):
  YES "Activate the scene Panik" → scene-agent: activate scene Panik
  NO  "Turn on the Ambiente in the living room" → light-agent, NOT scene-agent

- climate-agent (temperature, HVAC, fans, humidifiers, weather):
  YES "Set the living room to 22 degrees" → climate-agent: set living room to 22 degrees
  YES "Turn on the fan" → climate-agent (fans belong to climate)
  NO  "Turn on the ceiling lamp" → light-agent, NOT climate-agent

- music-agent (music playback, playlists, music volume):
  YES "Play jazz in the bedroom" → music-agent: play jazz in bedroom
  NO  "Turn on the TV" → media-agent, NOT music-agent
  NO  "Stop the music" → music-agent, NOT cancel-interaction

- media-agent (TVs, speakers, casting, media players):
  YES "Turn on the living room TV" → media-agent: turn on living room TV
  NO  "Play a playlist" → music-agent, NOT media-agent

- cover-agent (blinds, shutters, awnings, garage doors):
  YES "Open the office blind" → cover-agent: open office blind
  NO  "Turn on the awning lights" → light-agent (switching verb wins, Rule 1)

- vacuum-agent (robot vacuums):
  YES "Start the vacuum" → vacuum-agent: start vacuum
  NO  "Mop the floor" → general-agent (no agent can mop)

- timer-agent (timers, alarms, reminders, delayed actions):
  YES "Set a timer for 5 minutes" → timer-agent: set timer for 5 minutes
  NO  "What is on my calendar tomorrow?" → calendar-agent, NOT timer-agent

- calendar-agent (calendar events):
  YES "Add the dentist appointment for Friday" → calendar-agent: add dentist appointment for Friday
  NO  "Wake me at 7 am" → timer-agent, NOT calendar-agent

- lists-agent (todo and shopping lists):
  YES "Add milk to the shopping list" → lists-agent: add milk to shopping list
  NO  "What appointments do I have tomorrow?" → calendar-agent, NOT lists-agent

- security-agent (locks, alarms, cameras, door/window/motion sensors):
  YES "Is the front door locked?" → security-agent: check front door lock
  NO  "Close the office blind" → cover-agent, NOT security-agent

- automation-agent (Home Assistant automations):
  YES "Disable the night setback automation" → automation-agent: disable night setback automation
  NO  "Activate the scene Panik" → scene-agent, NOT automation-agent

- general-agent (fallback, Q&A, web search, conversation):
  YES "Who invented the light bulb?" → general-agent: inventor of light bulb
  NO  any clear device command → the matching specialized agent

- cancel-interaction (dismiss the assistant interaction):
  YES "Nevermind" → cancel-interaction: dismiss current interaction
  NO  "Stop the music" → music-agent, NOT cancel-interaction

SEQUENTIAL DELIVERY EXAMPLE:
- send-agent (delivery only, always the second line):
  YES "Write Anna that I will be late and send it to her"
      general-agent (90%): write message to Anna that I will be late
      send-agent (95%): send message to Anna
  NO  send-agent as the only line — delivery always needs a content agent first
END SEQUENTIAL DELIVERY EXAMPLE
